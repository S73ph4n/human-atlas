This is a modified version of the SPL Knee Atlas (knee-2016-09): cropped to the labelled knee, resampled to 0.5 mm isotropic voxels, reoriented to RAS, MRI intensities rescaled to 8 bits, and label values renumbered. It is not the original atlas. Research and education only; not for clinical use.
