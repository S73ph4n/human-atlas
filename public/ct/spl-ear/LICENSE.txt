This is a modified version of the SPL Inner Ear Atlas (inner-ear-2018-02): cropped to the middle and inner ear structures, resampled to 0.4 mm isotropic voxels, reoriented to RAS, CT values coded to 8 bits (piecewise-linear, finest in soft tissue), and label values renumbered. It is not the original atlas. Research and education only; not for clinical use.
